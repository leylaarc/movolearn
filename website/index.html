Lessons in reading, math, vocabulary, and discovery, built from the topics your child already likes, such as sharks, horses, space, dinosaurs, and whatever else they pick. A parent sets up the app. Kids just play.

No ads. No account. Progress stays on the device.

A short lesson, matched to your child

A parent picks a nickname, age, reading level, and math level. Then the child chooses topics. Each lesson uses those choices, not a one-size worksheet with a new sticker on top.

  1. Reading & words

    Stories, comprehension, and vocabulary with real subject words a ranger or scientist would use: tide chart, orbit, fossil, lava.

  2. Math in the story

    Counting, comparing, and problem-solving inside the same world: trays of snacks, boxes of rocks, or moons in a sky map.

  3. Discovery

    Science facts and a creative prompt so kids can draw what they learned, then celebrate and come back tomorrow.

Parents stay in the loop

Kids go from lesson to celebration, then home. The Learning Report lives in Profile, behind For Parents: short scores and recent lessons, without pulling children through an adult screen.

Setup once

A parent or guardian creates the profile. Children never sign in and never have a password.

Change interests anytime

Edit favorite topics from Home without resetting progress. New lessons follow the new list.

Learning stays on this iPhone or iPad.

This version does not create an account and does not send a child’s name, age, or lesson scores to Movo Learn servers. No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs.

Read the Privacy Policy

Profile and progress stay on the device. Reset in Profile, or delete the app, to erase them. The same policy is in the app under Profile → Privacy.

How a lesson moves

Reading, then math, then a discovery.

A parent picks a nickname, age, reading level, and math level. The child chooses topics. The lesson follows those choices, one part after another.

  1. Reading & words

    Stories, comprehension, and vocabulary with real subject words a ranger or scientist would use: tide chart, orbit, fossil, lava.

  2. Math in the story

    Counting, comparing, and problem-solving inside the same world: trays of snacks, boxes of rocks, or moons in a sky map.

  3. Discovery

    Science facts and a creative prompt so kids can draw what they learned, then celebrate and come back tomorrow.

For parents

Learning stays on this iPhone or iPad.

This version does not create an account and does not send a child’s name, age, or lesson scores to Movo Learn servers. No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs.

A parent creates the profile. Children never sign in. Favorite topics can change from Home without resetting progress. The Learning Report lives in Profile, behind For Parents.
